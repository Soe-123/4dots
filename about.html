Four Dots is a new project representing the collaboration between Joseph Field Eccles + Nicholas Smyth.

We deliver highly detailed cinematic soundscapes that bring your stories to life – whether it be features, shorts or commercials. Sound Design isn’t solely a technical tick box exercise – it is a powerful storytelling tool and one that we seek to maximise to its fullest potential.

Our latest project The Peril at Pincer Point, directed by Jake Kuhn + Noah Stratton-Twine had its World Premiere at SXSW, winning the NEON Auteur Award for Uncompromising Vision. As well as receiving write ups in Variety + Screen Daily. Our other work to date has been shown at film festivals around the world, including London + TriBeCa, and has additionally seen us both receive Grierson nominations.

So… need sound for your next project? Hit us up at hello@fourdotspost.com – we’d love to chat!
