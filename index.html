Four Dots represents the collaboration between Joseph Field Eccles and Nicholas Smyth. After graduating from the National Film & Television School in 2025, Joe & Nick have worked across a range of different projects from indie feature films to commercials. They are passionate about bringing stories to life through the creation of highly detailed, cinematic soundscapes. Their work to date has been screened at film festivals around the world, including London, Tribeca NY and most recently SXSW Film Festival in Austin, Texas. In addition to this, the pair have both previously been nominated for Grierson Awards.
